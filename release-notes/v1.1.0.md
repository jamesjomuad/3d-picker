# 3D Picker v1.1.0

This release overhauls the settings experience, turns the Selection Basket into its own tab, makes Fun Pick generation exhaustive, and fixes the rough edges reported on Android.

## ✨ New

### Settings, rebuilt
- Grouped panels: Appearance, Notifications, Data & Storage, Share & Updates and About.
- **Theme mode** — System, Light or Dark. Persisted on-device and applied across the whole app, tabs included.
- **Check for Update** — compares your installed version with the latest GitHub release and takes you straight to the download.
- **Clear App Data** — one confirmation away from a factory reset of boards, collection and settings.
- **Share 3D Picker** — the native Android share sheet.

### Collection tab
- Your Selection Basket is now a full tab (badge shows the count, too).
- Every saved pick carries the date and time it was added.
- Share your picks to any app, or delete them individually.

### Fun Pick, all the way up
- **Exhaustive generation** — every valid angle walk is now produced, not just a sample. Results render progressively with *Show more*.
- **Digit filters** — All, With 0, With 1, With 2.
- **L-shape pattern filters** — Г ㄱ ┘ L, matched on the path's geometry.
- **Sequence search** — comma-separated sequences, each found as a consecutive in-order run.
- **Add to Collection** — collect everything currently shown in one tap.

## 🔧 Improved
- Board cards: favorite star, relative "updated" time, Active badge, and a proper bottom sheet for the ⋯ options.
- Bottom tabs: new Collection list icon and Settings gear.
- Selected-cells insights: cells, digits, combinations and start counts.
- Foldable cards with smooth animations across selection, search and settings.

## 🐛 Fixed
- The Android navigation bar now stays visible (only the status bar is hidden).
- Duplicated boards appear in the library immediately.
- The board "⋯ More" sheet dismisses on outside tap, hardware back, or Cancel.
- The seeded Angle Board keeps its real digits (previously some devices persisted an all-zero grid; affected installs self-repair).
- Drag selection on the board: correct coordinates, no ScrollView interception.
